arp
===

File read
---------

[Sudo]
    arp -v -f /path/to/input-file
    Lines are likely leaked as error messages.

[SUID]
    arp -v -f /path/to/input-file
    Lines are likely leaked as error messages.

[Unprivileged]
    arp -v -f /path/to/input-file
    Lines are likely leaked as error messages.
