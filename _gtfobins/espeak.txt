espeak
======

File read
---------

[Sudo]
    espeak -qXf /path/to/input-file
    The file content appears in the middle of other textual information as phonemes.

[SUID]
    espeak -qXf /path/to/input-file
    The file content appears in the middle of other textual information as phonemes.

[Unprivileged]
    espeak -qXf /path/to/input-file
    The file content appears in the middle of other textual information as phonemes.
