time
====

Shell
-----

[Sudo]
    time /bin/sh
    Note that the shell might have its own builtin `time` implementation, which may behave differently than the binary, which is often located at `/usr/bin/time`.

[SUID]
    time /bin/sh -p
    Note that the shell might have its own builtin `time` implementation, which may behave differently than the binary, which is often located at `/usr/bin/time`.

[Unprivileged]
    time /bin/sh
    Note that the shell might have its own builtin `time` implementation, which may behave differently than the binary, which is often located at `/usr/bin/time`.
