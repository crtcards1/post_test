batcat
======

Inherit
-------

[Sudo]
    batcat --paging always /etc/hosts
    `--paging always` can be omitted provided that the output doesn't fit the screen.

[SUID]
    batcat --paging always /etc/hosts
    `--paging always` can be omitted provided that the output doesn't fit the screen.

[Unprivileged]
    batcat --paging always /etc/hosts
    `--paging always` can be omitted provided that the output doesn't fit the screen.
