unexpand
========

File read
---------

[Sudo]
    unexpand -t999 /path/to/input-file
    Convert sequences of (e.g., `999`) spaces to tab.

[SUID]
    unexpand -t999 /path/to/input-file
    Convert sequences of (e.g., `999`) spaces to tab.

[Unprivileged]
    unexpand -t999 /path/to/input-file
    Convert sequences of (e.g., `999`) spaces to tab.
