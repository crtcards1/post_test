octave
======

File read
---------

[Sudo]
    octave-cli --eval 'format none; fid = fopen("/path/to/input-file"); while(!feof(fid)); txt = fgetl(fid); disp(txt); endwhile; fclose(fid);'

[SUID]
    octave-cli --eval 'format none; fid = fopen("/path/to/input-file"); while(!feof(fid)); txt = fgetl(fid); disp(txt); endwhile; fclose(fid);'

[Unprivileged]
    octave-cli --eval 'format none; fid = fopen("/path/to/input-file"); while(!feof(fid)); txt = fgetl(fid); disp(txt); endwhile; fclose(fid);'

File write
----------

[Sudo]
    octave-cli --eval 'fid = fopen("/path/to/output-file", "w"); fputs(fid, "DATA"); fclose(fid);'

[SUID]
    octave-cli --eval 'fid = fopen("/path/to/output-file", "w"); fputs(fid, "DATA"); fclose(fid);'

[Unprivileged]
    octave-cli --eval 'fid = fopen("/path/to/output-file", "w"); fputs(fid, "DATA"); fclose(fid);'
